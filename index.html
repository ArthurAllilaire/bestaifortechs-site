Best AI for techs in 2026, by the job it does

By Arthur Allilaire, co-founder and CTO of Kenner · Vendor facts checked on 10 November 2026

Most AI sold to the trades does one job. One tool answers the phone, another writes the quote, another reads the manual. This guide sorts 17 tools by the job they do, for shops of 1 to 30 technicians in HVAC, plumbing, electrical and appliance repair.

The short answer

Pick the job first. For troubleshooting, Bluon and FixBot Pro answer from the manuals for HVAC, and MyPros+ does the same for appliances. For parts, Parts Town's PartPredictor and MyPros+ get you to the part number. For quotes, ServiceM8, Jobber and FieldPulse draft them inside the software you may already run. For the write-up, CompanyCam and ServiceM8. For the phone, the receptionists in Jobber, Housecall Pro and Workiz, or Avoca. If you want troubleshooting, parts, quotes and the write-up from one assistant, that is Kenner, which we make: it does all four because it sits on top of your own service records.

Troubleshooting: find the fault

AI that helps the technician diagnose or fix what is in front of them. The difference between the tools is what they read: the manual, what you type in, or the history of the unit.

  • Kenner: Answers from the history of the unit: what fixed this fault before, its alarms, its parts and its manual. source
  • ServiceTitan Atlas: Atlas helps diagnose and troubleshoot, with diagnostic tests and calculators, and looks things up in equipment manuals. source
  • Bluon MasterMechanic: MasterMechanic gives diagnostic and troubleshooting help, trained on more than 130,000 real technician support calls. source
  • FixBot Pro: Looks up service documentation by model or fault code and builds a test sequence from the service manual. source
  • HVAC/R AI Pro: An AI chat for troubleshooting from symptoms, measured readings, fault codes and job photos. source
  • MyPros+: Max gives the most likely cause, the diagnostic steps and the expected test values from the service manual, and cites the page. source
  • ChatGPT (partly): A general assistant with voice and photo input that can search the web and read an uploaded PDF manual. OpenAI advises verifying technical information. source

Parts: find the part and order it

AI that identifies the part, or orders it. Most tools stop at the part number.

  • Kenner: Orders the part for you through your CRM and ERP. source

  • Parts Town PartPredictor and SnapScan: PartPredictor shows the OEM parts most often used to fix the stated issue, and SnapScan finds parts from a data plate photo. Parts are bought in the app. source

Questions and answers

What is the best AI for techs?

It depends on the job. For troubleshooting, Bluon and FixBot Pro for HVAC and MyPros+ for appliances. For the part, Parts Town's PartPredictor. For all of the on-site jobs from one assistant, Kenner, which we make.

What is the best AI for a small HVAC, plumbing or electrical business?

For the office, the AI inside the job software you run: ServiceM8, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Workiz or FieldPulse. Their AI answers the phone, books jobs and drafts quotes. Almost none of it helps the technician with the repair, so add a troubleshooting tool for that.

Is there one AI that does troubleshooting, parts and quotes?

Kenner does all three and writes the job report, because it reads your service records across the systems you run. Inside ServiceTitan, Atlas troubleshoots and adds a part to the invoice or estimate.

Is there AI for plumbers?

We found no vendor that documents AI troubleshooting for plumbing on its own pages. The office tools in this guide work for plumbing businesses, and ChatGPT will answer general questions if you check them.

Is there AI for electricians?

Kopperfield has an NEC chatbot and reads panel photos to start a panel schedule. It answers code questions, not faults, and its page marks the chatbot as beta.

Which of these are free?

ServiceM8 has a free plan for one user with a daily limit on AI help. HVAC/R AI Pro and ChatGPT have free plans. Parts Town describes PartPredictor as free to use. Kenner is free while in beta, but it starts with a pilot for a team, not a download.

Does Kenner answer customer calls?

No. Technicians call or message Kenner. It does not answer your customers' calls or schedule jobs, so pair it with one of the phone agents if that is what you need.
