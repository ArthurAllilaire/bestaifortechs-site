Best AI for Techs

AI for finding and ordering parts

By Arthur Allilaire, co-founder and CTO of Kenner · Vendor facts checked on 10 November 2026

The short answer

This is the thinnest job in the guide. Two tools get you to the part number, two more help in part, and one orders the part for you.

  • Parts Town PartPredictor and SnapScan: PartPredictor shows the OEM parts most often used to fix the stated issue, and SnapScan finds parts from a data plate photo. Parts are bought in the app.
  • MyPros+: Max and the parts diagrams give the part number for the model. Stock checks and ordering inside the app are not stated.
  • Kenner: Orders the part for you through your CRM and ERP. Our product.
  • Bluon MasterMechanic: Bluon provides parts for HVAC units by model number. Availability checks and ordering are not stated on its pages.
  • ServiceTitan Atlas: Atlas adds a part to the invoice or estimate. Availability checks and ordering are not stated on its pages.

Finding the part

Parts Town's PartPredictor shows the OEM parts most often used for a stated issue, and SnapScan reads the data plate from a photo. Parts Town says it is not a diagnostic tool. MyPros+ gives the part number from the manual for appliance repair.

Ordering it

Parts Town sells the parts it finds. MyPros+ gives a number to copy into an order. Kenner orders the part for you through your CRM and ERP, which is why it needs to be connected to them first.

Questions and answers

Can AI tell me which part has failed?

That is troubleshooting, not parts lookup. Parts Town says PartPredictor cannot tell you what is wrong with the equipment. Use a troubleshooting tool first.

Is there AI that checks stock at my supplier?

None of the pages we checked describes that for a contractor's own supplier accounts, so we do not claim it.
