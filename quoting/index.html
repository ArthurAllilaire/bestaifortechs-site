Best AI for quotes and estimates

By Arthur Allilaire, co-founder and CTO of Kenner · Vendor facts checked on 10 November 2026

The short answer

Four tools draft the quote in full and three more help with part of it. They differ in what they price from: your own item list, supplier prices by location, or the service record of the job.

  • ServiceM8 AI features: AI Automated Quoting drafts the quote description and a list of materials and services.
  • Jobber AI: Jobber creates draft quotes automatically from new requests, or with the Draft for me button, for you to review and send.
  • FieldPulse Field Intelligence: Maverick drafts an estimate from a typed or dictated request using your item list and prices, and you approve it.
  • Handoff: The AI builds an estimate from a photo, floor plan or quick scope, priced by location, and generates the proposal.
  • Kenner: Prepares the quote for you through your CRM and ERP. Our product.
  • ServiceTitan Atlas: Atlas adds a part to the invoice or estimate. Writing or pricing the whole estimate is not stated in the fact sheet.
  • CompanyCam AI: The Crew plan lists creating proposals using voice notes and photos. The page does not say that AI writes or prices them.

Service work or project work

Handoff is built for remodels and installs: it prices an estimate from a photo, a floor plan or a typed scope. ServiceM8, Jobber and FieldPulse draft the quote inside the job software, from the request or from your own price list.

Kenner prepares the quote from the service record through your CRM and ERP, so it follows from the diagnosis instead of starting from a blank form.

You still approve it

Every one of these drafts for a person to review. Jobber's quotes wait for you to review and send, FieldPulse's Maverick asks for approval, and ServiceM8 drafts the description and the list of materials.

Questions and answers

Can AI price a job from a photo?

Handoff says it builds a priced estimate from a photo, a floor plan or a quick scope, for residential construction. For service and repair, the tools here draft from your request and your price list.

Which quoting AI publishes its price?

ServiceM8, Jobber and Handoff do. FieldPulse quotes on request.
