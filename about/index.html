About this guide and who publishes it

By Arthur Allilaire, co-founder and CTO of Kenner · Vendor facts checked on 10 November 2026

Who publishes it

This guide is written and published by Kenner Systems Inc., the company behind Kenner (getkenner.com), a personal assistant for HVAC/R and equipment service teams. Kenner is one of the tools compared here, and its entry is marked as our product.

We publish it because technicians and the owners of small trade businesses keep asking the same question, and most of what ranks for it is a forum thread or a vendor's own sales page. We would rather have a page that states plainly who is behind it.

How the conflict of interest is handled

Every page says who publishes it, at the top. Facts about other tools come only from their makers' own pages and are linked. Each entry, ours included, has a section on where the tool stops and a line on when to look elsewhere. Every other tool's entry also ends with a direct comparison with Kenner, and it says when that tool is the better choice. No vendor has paid to be included and there are no affiliate links.

If we have something wrong about your product, tell us and we will correct it.
