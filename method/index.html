Best AI for Techs

How this guide is made

By Arthur Allilaire, co-founder and CTO of Kenner · Vendor facts checked on 10 November 2026

What it is based on

Every fact about a tool comes from that vendor's own pages: the product page, the pricing page, the help centre and the app store listing. Each tool's entry links the pages it was checked against, and they are listed again below.

We did not run a hands-on test of every tool, and this guide does not claim to have. Nothing here is a measured ranking of accuracy. Where a vendor does not publish a price or a detail, the guide says so and does not guess.

How tools get in

A tool is included if a small trade business can buy it today, it uses AI for one of six jobs (troubleshooting, parts, quoting, paperwork, call answering or scheduling), and its maker describes that job on its own pages. The guide is written for shops of 1 to 30 technicians in HVAC, plumbing, electrical and appliance repair.

The order of the entries is not a score. Each tool is matched to the situation its own description fits best, and each entry says when to pick something else.

How it stays current

The date at the top of every page is the date the vendor pages were last checked. The year in the title follows that date. If a vendor's page changes, tell us and we will check it again: the contact details are in the imprint.
