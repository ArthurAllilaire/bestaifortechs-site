Best AI for Techs

AI call answering for trade businesses

By Arthur Allilaire, co-founder and CTO of Kenner · Vendor facts checked on 10 November 2026

The short answer

Most small-shop software now has a phone agent, and Avoca sells one on its own. This is office AI. Kenner, which we make, does not do it.

  • Jobber AI: Receptionist answers calls and texts 24/7, books visits, takes requests and escalates to you when needed.
  • Housecall Pro AI Team: CSR AI answers calls and chats 24/7 and books jobs. Text answering is in alpha.
  • Workiz Genius: Genius Answering answers calls, emails, texts, web forms and lead sources 24/7 and books jobs.
  • FieldPulse Field Intelligence: Operator AI answers after-hours, declined or overloaded calls and books jobs directly into FieldPulse.
  • ServiceM8 AI features: Phone Agent answers calls for you. Messages and booking requests arrive in your Inbox for review.
  • Avoca: Avoca's Inbound AI answers calls, texts and chats 24/7 and books jobs in the conversation.

Answering is not the same as booking

Jobber, Housecall Pro, Workiz, FieldPulse and Avoca say their agents book the job. ServiceM8's Phone Agent sends messages and booking requests to your inbox for review.

Assigning the technician is rarer. Only Workiz and FieldPulse describe choosing who goes.

What it costs

Jobber's Receptionist is a $29 a month add-on. Housecall Pro sells CSR AI separately and does not publish its price. Workiz, FieldPulse and Avoca quote on request.

Questions and answers

Does an AI receptionist help my technicians?

No. It helps the office. None of the phone agents here describes anything for the technician on the job.
